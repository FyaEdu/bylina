Chapter One

What is a Bylina?

A bylina (plural: byliny) is a traditional East Slavic epic song — a narrative poem once sung rather than read, carried from voice to voice for centuries before anyone wrote it down.

Byliny tell of bogatyrs, heroic warrior-guardians who defend the land and its people, alongside princes, monsters, and ordinary travellers. The stories blend real historical memory — old cities, real threats at the frontier — with legend, exaggeration, and wonder.

They belonged to an oral tradition: performed by singer-storytellers, often to the accompaniment of a stringed instrument, and reshaped a little with every telling. What survives today comes from versions collected by folklorists in the 19th and 20th centuries, mostly from tellers in the Russian North.

Chapter Four

Alyosha Popovich

The youngest of the three great bogatyrs, and a priest's son by birth — his name comes from pop, meaning priest.

Where Ilya Muromets is known for steady strength and Dobrynya Nikitich for noble skill, Alyosha's byliny prize his wit. He is quick to size up a situation, willing to bend the rules of a fair fight, and unafraid to use trickery when brute force would fail.

He appears in several tales, most famously in his confrontation with the monstrous Tugarin Zmeyevich, which you'll experience in the next chapter.

Chapter Eight

From Folklore to Fantasy

Old folklore does not stay still. Across the centuries, bylina motifs — heroic warriors, monstrous foes, journeys through wild lands — have resurfaced in new stories, reimagined for new audiences.

Traditional Bylina

Oral epic songs, performed by named and unnamed folk singers, collected and written down by folklorists starting in the 19th century. Rooted in specific regional traditions and historical memory.

Modern Fantasy Interpretation

Contemporary games, films, and books that borrow atmosphere, character types, or imagery from folklore traditions like bylina — reworked freely as fiction, not historical record.

These are two different things — inspiration is not equivalence.

Chapter Nine

Modern Echo

Some modern fantasy settings draw on Slavic-inspired atmosphere — snowy landscapes, folk-costume silhouettes, epic tones. The video game Genshin Impact's region of Snezhnaya is one widely-known example of this kind of borrowing.

This is a case of modern fantasy reinterpreting cultural imagery and atmosphere — not a retelling of any specific bylina, and not a claim that any fictional character literally is a traditional bogatyr like Alyosha Popovich.

Traditional Folklore

  • Specific named heroes with documented traditional stories
  • Transmitted orally, then recorded by folklorists
  • Rooted in a particular historical and regional context

Modern Fantasy

  • Original fictional characters and worlds
  • Created by contemporary writers, artists and designers
  • Free to borrow mood and imagery without historical obligation
